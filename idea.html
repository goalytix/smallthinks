Game 3 ideas: adding & taking away ➕➖

Five ways to teach adding and taking away to kids aged 4–7. Each picture shows one round.

🐸 numhop my pick

The frog starts on 5. Tap "hop" 3 times and it hops 6, 7, 8 while the voice counts along. Taking away means hopping backward.

Teaches: the number line

🎈 numpop

Seven balloons float up and the voice says "Pop 3!". Pop, pop, pop. Now count what's left. For adding, tap to blow up more balloons.

Teaches: counting real things

👾 numchomp

"I want 8 cookies! I have 5." Tap cookies onto the plate until there are 8, then the monster munches. Too many? It burps.

Teaches: "how many more?"

💨 numpair

A numfade remix. Tap two numbers that add up to 10, like 3 and 7, and they whoosh away. Kids already know how it works.

Teaches: pairs that make 10

🚂 numtrain

The train has 10 seats in two rows of 5. Four kids get on, then 2 more. How many now? The train toots when it's right.

Teaches: groups of 5 and 10

Three sizes

Easy (up to 5), Medium (up to 10) or Big (up to 20).

🤗

Kind mistakes

A wrong answer just wobbles and says "Try again!". No buzzers, no lost lives.

⭐

Stars, then confetti

A star for each right answer, then a party after 10.

🔊

Read aloud

Every question is spoken, so kids who can't read yet can still play.
